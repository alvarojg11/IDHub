  Rationale

<div className="mx-auto max-w-5xl">
  <p className="text-lg leading-6 text-gray-800 mb-6 text-justify">
    This patient should start ART promptly with a potent, high-barrier regimen that is
    practical in the setting of chronic hemodialysis. Integrase inhibitor-based therapy
    is preferred for most treatment-naive adults. In this case, dolutegravir plus
    TAF/FTC is selected as the preferred initial regimen, with administration after
    dialysis on dialysis days.
  </p>

  <p className="text-lg leading-6 text-gray-800 mb-6 text-justify">
    A common pitfall is selecting fixed-dose combinations that include nucleoside analogs
    requiring renal adjustment in ways that are incompatible with the fixed tablet.
    Another practical issue is polycation interactions. Cation-containing phosphate binders
    or supplements (such as calcium, magnesium, iron, aluminum, or lanthanum products)
    can reduce integrase inhibitor absorption, so timing separation should be reviewed
    explicitly at ART initiation.
  </p>

  <p className="text-lg leading-6 text-gray-800 mb-6 text-justify">
    Hepatitis B status is a high-priority baseline assessment. If HBV coinfection is
    present, ART should preserve two HBV-active drugs in the regimen to avoid HBV
    breakthrough or flare. Follow-up should include early virologic response checks,
    adherence review, and resistance-result reconciliation once genotype data return.
