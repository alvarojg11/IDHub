## Key teaching points (pathophysiology)

- Nephrotic syndrome = secondary/acquired immunodeficiency via urinary loss of immune effector proteins.
- **IgG loss** → reduced antibody-mediated opsonization (low serum IgG is the biochemical fingerprint).
- **Alternative complement Factor B and Factor D loss** (filtered + renal catabolism) → depleted amplification C3 convertase → impaired opsonization of encapsulated organisms.
- Encapsulated organisms predominate: *S. pneumoniae* (archetype — capsule resists classical lysis, depends on opsonization), *H. influenzae*, capsulated enteric GNR.
- Additional contributors: transferrin loss (↓iron-binding, ↑available iron), zinc deficiency, edema as culture medium, iatrogenic immunosuppression (steroids/CNI/rituximab).
- Distinct from asplenia, CGD (catalase-positive organisms), iron overload (siderophilic: Vibrio/Listeria), HIV (T-cell).
- Prevention: pneumococcal vaccination (PCV15+PPSV23 or PCV20) timed to remission/before rituximab + achieve remission to halt the immune-protein leak. IVIG and lifelong penicillin prophylaxis are NOT standard.
