# HPV yeast HistorID source notes

## Sources checked

1. Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2008
   URL: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/medicine/2008/summary/
   Key details: strongest concise source for the discovery-level hinge. Explicitly states that Harald zur Hausen received the prize for the discovery of human papillomaviruses causing cervical cancer.

2. European Medicines Agency, `Gardasil`
   URL: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/gardasil
   Key details: strongest mechanism source available in this environment. States clearly that Gardasil contains purified L1 proteins, that these are produced in yeast cells by recombinant DNA technology, and that the proteins assemble into virus-like particles that cannot cause infection.

3. U.S. Food and Drug Administration, `Gardasil`
   URL: https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/vaccines/gardasil
   Key details: useful regulatory framing for the recombinant quadrivalent vaccine and its approved indications.

4. Encyclopaedia Britannica, `human papillomavirus`
   URL: https://www.britannica.com/science/human-papillomavirus
   Key details: broad HPV context, including cervical cancer relevance and vaccine prevention.

5. Wikimedia Commons, `Gardasil.jpg`
   URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gardasil.jpg
   Key details: usable hero image with clear vaccine labeling. License CC BY-SA 4.0. Better for a balanced HPV-vaccine story than a generic yeast micrograph alone.

6. Wikimedia Commons, `Saccharomyces cerevisiae.jpg`
   URL: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Saccharomyces_cerevisiae.jpg
   Key details: available fallback image if we later decide to make the social hook even more yeast-centered.

## Drafting notes

- The best hook is the decoy idea: the vaccine uses a shell without the genome.
- Keep the science words in place: `Saccharomyces cerevisiae`, `L1 proteins`, `virus-like particles`, `recombinant DNA technology`.
- Avoid overstating the role of yeast as if the vaccine itself is yeast. More accurate: yeast cells were used to produce the L1 proteins.
- Keep it balanced: zur Hausen for the cancer link, then the yeast/VLP platform as the practical breakthrough.
