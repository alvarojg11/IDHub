# Pure Neuritic Leprosy: Differential Diagnosis Papers
## Literature Search — 2026-06-09
### Query: "pure neural leprosy differential diagnosis CIDP mononeuritis multiplex"

---

## 1. Nascimento OJM. Leprosy neuropathy: clinical presentations.
- **Journal:** Arquivos de Neuro-Psiquiatria
- **Year:** 2013 (Sep)
- **DOI:** 10.1590/0004-282X20130146
- **PMID:** 24141500
- **Key Findings:** Classic review of leprosy neuropathy clinical presentations. Mononeuritis, mononeuritis multiplex (MM), and polyneuritis (MM summation) are the most frequent patterns. Emphasizes early detection and MDT as most important steps in preventing deformity and disability.

## 2. AlOtaibi MK, AlQahtani BG, AlQawahmed RM. Unmasking the mimic: Leprosy neuropathy misdiagnosed as chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy.
- **Journal:** Neurosciences (Riyadh, Saudi Arabia)
- **Year:** 2025 (Apr)
- **DOI:** 10.17712/nsj.2025.2.20240057
- **PMID:** 40199529
- **Key Findings:** Case report of 72-year-old Saudi woman misdiagnosed with CIDP based on nerve conduction studies. Treated with IVIG without improvement. Sural nerve and skin biopsies confirmed leprosy. Demonstrates leprosy neuropathy can perfectly mimic CIDP, especially in non-endemic areas.

## 3. Ranjan A, Kumar A, Sinha N, Kumar A. Leprosy Presenting as Distal Acquired Demyelinating Symmetric Variant of CIDP.
- **Journal:** International Journal of Mycobacteriology
- **Year:** 2024 (Apr)
- **DOI:** 10.4103/ijmy.ijmy_39_24
- **PMID:** 38916395
- **Key Findings:** 46-year-old female presented with progressive weakness, tingling, and numbness. NCS showed demyelination. Slit-skin smears confirmed leprosy with AFB. Demonstrates leprosy can present as the DADS variant of CIDP.

## 4. Jardim M, Vital RT, Illarramendi X, Hacker M, Junqueira B, Pitta IJR, et al. The red flags of ulnar neuropathy in leprosy.
- **Journal:** PLoS ONE
- **Year:** 2021
- **DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pone.0259804
- **PMID:** 34797866
- **Key Findings:** Identifies ulnar nerve conduction characteristics to aid in differential diagnosis of ulnar neuropathy in leprosy vs non-leprosy etiology. PNL diagnosis is challenging because patients share characteristics with common conditions like ulnar compression neuropathy.

## 5. Cotti Piccinelli S, Tagliapietra M, Cavallaro T, Labella B, Risi B, Caria F, et al. Leprosy Neuropathy in a Non-Endemic Area: A Clinical and Pathological Study.
- **Journal:** Biomedicines
- **Year:** 2023 (Sep)
- **DOI:** 10.3390/biomedicines11092468
- **PMID:** 37760909
- **Key Findings:** Italian study in non-endemic area. Mononeuropathies, multiple mononeuropathies, and polyneuropathies can present in PNL form. Differential diagnosis remains challenging due to rarity and overlapping features with other neuropathies.

## 6. Sadhukhan S, Bhardwaj A, Kumar S, Yadav T, Patra S, Tiwari S. Rare Masqueraders of Pure Neuritic Leprosy: A Report of Four Cases.
- **Journal:** Indian Dermatology Online Journal
- **Year:** 2025 (Mar-Apr)
- **DOI:** 10.4103/idoj.idoj_495_24
- **PMID:** 40125031
- **Key Findings:** Four cases of disorders mimicking PNL. Various conditions can clinically mimic leprosy neuropathy, and patients may receive MDT unnecessarily. Emphasizes the importance of thorough differential diagnosis.

## 7. Santos LOD, Fernandes TRMO, Barbosa TRS, Batista JADS, Souza CDF. Pure neural leprosy or amyloid neuropathy? Systematic review and clinical case report.
- **Journal:** Revista da Associacao Medica Brasileira
- **Year:** 2021 (Jan)
- **DOI:** 10.1590/1806-9282.67.01.20200422
- **PMID:** 34161469
- **Key Findings:** Reports a case initially suspected as PNL but ultimately diagnosed as amyloid neuropathy. Systematic review of differential diagnoses that mimic PNL. Highlights the diagnostic challenge.

## 8. Huda S, Krishnan A. An unusual cause of mononeuritis multiplex.
- **Journal:** Practical Neurology
- **Year:** 2013 (Feb)
- **DOI:** 10.1136/practneurol-2012-000343
- **PMID:** 23315459
- **Key Findings:** Case report demonstrating leprosy as a cause of mononeuritis multiplex pattern — important teaching point for differential diagnosis.

## 9. Sumangala S, Nikfekr E, George J, Holmes CW. Leprosy neuropathy masquerading as cellulitis.
- **Journal:** Postgraduate Medical Journal
- **Year:** 2019 (Apr)
- **DOI:** 10.1136/postgradmedj-2019-136392
- **PMID:** 30918118
- **Key Findings:** Demonstrates yet another atypical presentation of leprosy neuropathy.

## 10. Sindic CJ. Infectious neuropathies.
- **Journal:** Current Opinion in Neurology
- **Year:** 2013 (Oct)
- **DOI:** 10.1097/WCO.0b013e328364c036
- **PMID:** 23945279
- **Key Findings:** Review of infectious causes of neuropathy including leprosy. Places PNL in the broader context of infectious neuropathy differential diagnosis.

## 11. Ooi WW, Srinivasan J. Leprosy and the peripheral nervous system: basic and clinical aspects.
- **Journal:** Muscle & Nerve
- **Year:** 2004
- **DOI:** 10.1002/mus.20113
- **PMID:** 15372437
- **Key Findings:** M. leprae has predilection for Schwann cells, resulting in destruction of myelin, secondary inflammatory changes, and destruction of nerve architecture. Cardinal diagnostic features and differential diagnosis reviewed.

## 12. Bathina A, Kollipara H, Sravani G, Laxmi VS. Beyond Classic Leprosy: Exploring Atypical Manifestations and Their Diagnostic Challenges.
- **Journal:** Cureus
- **Year:** 2024
- **DOI:** 10.7759/cureus.75957
- **PMID:** 39834975
- **Key Findings:** Explores atypical manifestations in 15 biopsy-confirmed patients. Addresses the wide array of conditions that can mimic or be mimicked by leprosy.
