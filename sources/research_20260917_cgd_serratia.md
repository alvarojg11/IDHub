## Key teaching facts (cross-checked)

- CGD = defective phagocyte NADPH oxidase (NOX2) complex; X-linked CYBB/gp91phox
  ~2/3 of cases; autosomal recessive NCF1/p47phox most common AR form in US/Europe.
- Organism spectrum = catalase-positive: S. aureus, Serratia, Burkholderia cepacia
  complex ("Burkholderia cepacia syndrome" — fulminant pneumonia/sepsis), Nocardia,
  mycobacteria incl. BCG, Scedosporium, Aspergillus (leading cause of death).
- Catalase-negative organisms (pneumococcus, streptococci) are uncommon causes of
  CGD disease — their own H2O2 can be used by CGD phagocytes (MPO pathway).
- DHR 123 flow cytometry: PMA-stimulated oxidation of DHR to rhodamine 123;
  absent shift in X-CGD, residual activity in AR forms, bimodal histogram in
  X-linked carriers (lyonization). NBT slide test = historical.
- Serratia microbiology: AmpC β-lactamase; amoxicillin-clavulanate and 3rd-gen
  cephalosporins unreliable (vignette treatment failure); use cefepime,
  carbapenem, or fluoroquinolone. Prodigiosin red pigment temperature-regulated,
  often absent at 37 °C.
- Prophylaxis backbone: TMP-SMX + itraconazole ± IFN-γ; HSCT curative
  (RIC, HLA-matched); lentiviral gene therapy emerging.

## Case structure

- Q1 diagnosis → NADPH oxidase defect (distractors: IL-12/IFN-γ axis MSMD,
  STAT3 hyper-IgE, terminal complement deficiency, cyclic neutropenia)
- Q2 testing → DHR flow cytometry (distractors: NBT, bone marrow morphology,
  CYBB sequencing alone, quantitative immunoglobulins)
- Q3 management → TMP-SMX + itraconazole ± IFN-γ + HSCT referral (distractors:
  IVIG, chronic G-CSF, TMP-SMX alone, observation)
- Registry slug: cgd-serratia-pneumonia; published 2026-09-17.
