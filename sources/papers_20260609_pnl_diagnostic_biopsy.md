# Pure Neuritic Leprosy: Histopathological Diagnosis Papers
## Literature Search — 2026-06-09
### Query: "pure neuritic leprosy diagnostic criteria nerve biopsy granuloma"

---

## 1. Razdan N, V B, Sadhu S. Pure neuritic leprosy: Latest advancements and diagnostic modalities.
- **Journal:** Diagnostic Microbiology and Infectious Disease
- **Year:** 2024 (Dec)
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.diagmicrobio.2024.116529
- **PMID:** 39278136
- **Key Findings:** Comprehensive review of PNL diagnosis. Gold standard remains histopathological examination of peripheral nerve biopsy. Detection of bacteria is difficult even on biopsy; histological findings may be non-specific. Reviews newer diagnostic modalities beyond biopsy including serological and molecular methods.

## 2. Rao PN, Suneetha S. Pure neuritic leprosy: Current status and relevance.
- **Journal:** Indian Journal of Dermatology, Venereology and Leprology
- **Year:** 2016 (May-Jun)
- **DOI:** 10.4103/0378-6323.179086
- **PMID:** 27088926
- **Key Findings:** Landmark review paper. PNL recognized as distinct subgroup by Indian Association of Leprologists. Cases reported from Asia, Africa, South America, and Europe. Constitutes a significant percentage of leprosy cases in India. Addresses clinical and management ambiguities.

## 3. Hui M, Uppin MS, Challa S, Meena AK, Kaul S. Pure neuritic leprosy: Resolving diagnostic issues in acid fast bacilli (AFB)-negative nerve biopsies.
- **Journal:** Annals of Indian Academy of Neurology
- **Year:** 2015 (Jul-Sep)
- **DOI:** 10.4103/0972-2327.162284
- **PMID:** 26425006
- **Key Findings:** Critical paper for AFB-negative biopsies. In absence of granuloma or lepra bacilli, other morphologic changes in endoneurium and perineurium can support a probable PNL diagnosis. Proposes diagnostic criteria for AFB-negative nerve biopsies including perineurial thickening, endoneurial inflammation, and fibrosis.

## 4. Shukla B, Verma R, Kumar V, Kumar M, Malhotra KP, Garg RK, et al. Pathological, ultrasonographic, and electrophysiological characterization of clinically diagnosed cases of pure neuritic leprosy.
- **Journal:** Journal of the Peripheral Nervous System
- **Year:** 2020 (Jun)
- **DOI:** 10.1111/jns.12372
- **PMID:** 32250546
- **Key Findings:** Prospective study of 100 consecutive PNL patients correlating ultrasonographic and biopsy findings. Many PNL patients are started on drug therapy without confirmation of diagnosis. Demonstrates the value of combining ultrasound, electrophysiology, and nerve biopsy for diagnostic confirmation.

## 5. Sandhu S, Raman DK, Sood A, Sinha P, Singh PY, Dixit PK. Cytomorphological Patterns of Nerve Aspirates in Pure Neuritic Leprosy.
- **Journal:** Journal of Cytology
- **Year:** 2021 (Jan-Mar)
- **DOI:** 10.4103/JOC.JOC_182_20
- **PMID:** 33935387
- **Key Findings:** Describes nerve aspirate cytomorphology patterns in PNL, offering a less invasive alternative to full nerve biopsy for diagnosis.

## 6. Payne R, Baccon J, Dossett J, Scollard D, Byler D, Patel A, et al. Pure neuritic leprosy presenting as ulnar nerve neuropathy.
- **Journal:** Journal of Neurosurgery
- **Year:** 2015 (Nov)
- **DOI:** 10.3171/2014.9.JNS142210
- **PMID:** 26047418
- **Key Findings:** Case report from the US demonstrating electrodiagnostic, radiographic, and histopathological findings in PNL presenting as isolated ulnar nerve neuropathy. Important for neurosurgeons to consider PNL in the differential of peripheral neuropathy.

## 7. de Athaide MM, Leal-Calvo T, Da Silva TP, et al. Gene expression profiling in pure neural leprosy: insights into pathogenesis and diagnostic biomarkers.
- **Journal:** Frontiers in Immunology
- **Year:** 2025
- **DOI:** (see PMID 40421009)
- **PMID:** 40421009
- **Key Findings:** Transcriptomic profiling reveals unique gene expression changes in PNL nerves, shedding light on immune response and pathogenesis. Potential diagnostic biomarkers identified for PNL.

## 8. Kumar B, Kaur I, Dogra S, Kumaran MS. Pure neuritic leprosy in India: an appraisal.
- **Journal:** International Journal of Leprosy and Other Mycobacterial Diseases
- **Year:** 2004 (Sep)
- **DOI:** 10.1489/0020-7349(2004)72<284:PNLIIA>2.0.CO;2
- **PMID:** 15485285
- **Key Findings:** Large series appraisal of PNL from India, establishing clinical patterns and epidemiological significance.

## 9. Forgione P, Barabino G, Cavalchini A, Clapasson A, Reni L, Parodi A. Pure neuritic leprosy.
- **Journal:** Giornale Italiano di Dermatologia e Venereologia
- **Year:** 2018 (Feb)
- **DOI:** 10.23736/S0392-0488.16.05474-2
- **PMID:** 29319286
- **Key Findings:** Italian report demonstrating PNL occurs outside endemic regions, addressing diagnostic challenges in non-endemic settings.
