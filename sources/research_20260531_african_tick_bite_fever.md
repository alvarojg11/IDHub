## African Tick-Bite Fever Research Notes

Date: 2026-05-31
Slug: `african-tick-bite-fever`
Title: `Legs from the Bush`

### Clinical anchors used for the case

- Organism: `Rickettsia africae`
- Typical setting: rural or safari travel in sub-Saharan Africa
- Key clues: fever, headache, myalgias, one or more inoculation eschars, regional lymphadenopathy
- Distinguishing clue: multiple eschars are common in African tick-bite fever
- Early diagnosis: PCR from eschar swab or biopsy outperforms acute serology in the first week
- Treatment: doxycycline, started empirically when suspected
