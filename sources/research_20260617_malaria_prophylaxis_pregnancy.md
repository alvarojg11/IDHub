# Research Notes — Malaria Chemoprophylaxis in Pregnancy

**Case slug:** malaria-prophylaxis-pregnancy
**Date:** 2026-06-17
**Working title:** "The Border She Couldn't Cross"

---

## Clinical scenario
A 29-year-old primigravida at 20 weeks' gestation plans a 7-week trip:
- Kenya and Tanzania (3 wk, rural/safari) — chloroquine-resistant P. falciparum
- India (2 wk, rural and urban) — chloroquine-resistant P. falciparum + P. vivax
- Thai–Cambodian border (2 wk) — mefloquine-resistant P. falciparum

No chronic meds, no G6PD deficiency, no psychiatric/cardiac history.

## Key decisions
1. **Thai–Cambodian border leg** → advise against; no effective + pregnancy-safe prophylaxis.
2. **East African + Indian legs** → mefloquine (only CDC/WHO-recommended agent for chloroquine-resistant areas in pregnancy, all trimesters).
3. **P. vivax/ovale radical cure** → defer to postpartum after G6PD; 8-aminoquinolines contraindicated in pregnancy.

## Drug-by-drug in pregnancy

| Drug | Pregnancy status | Reason |
|------|------------------|--------|
| Mefloquine | ✓ Recommended (all trimesters) | Only effective option for chloroquine-resistant areas; large safety studies (>30k exposures) |
| Chloroquine | Safe but ineffective here | P. falciparum resistance in Africa/India |
| Atovaquone-proguanil | Not recommended | Insufficient safety data |
| Doxycycline | Contraindicated | Fetal teeth/bone effects |
| Primaquine | Contraindicated | Fetal G6PD hemolysis (status unknown in utero) |
| Tafenoquine | Contraindicated | Fetal G6PD hemolysis; long half-life |

## Mefloquine specifics
- Start 2 wk before travel (loading + detect neuropsychiatric side effects).
- Weekly during travel; continue 4 wk after leaving.
- Screen for psychiatric disease, seizures, cardiac conduction abnormalities.
- FDA removed first-trimester prophylactic-use contraindication in 2022.
- Meta-analysis (Tan 2024, J Travel Med) + Schlagenhauf 2012 (CID): no ↑ congenital anomalies, stillbirth, fetal death.

## Mefloquine resistance (Greater Mekong)
- Documented: Thai–Cambodian border, Thai–Myanmar border, and across the Greater Mekong subregion (Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam, southern China).
- With mefloquine being the only pregnancy-safe option, no chemoprophylaxis remains for these zones → advise against travel / defer / re-route.
- Stand-by self-treatment (SBET) NOT recommended for pregnant travelers; needs prompt professional evaluation of any febrile illness.

## Radical cure (anti-relapse) — P. vivax / P. ovale
- Hypnozoites (dormant liver forms) cause relapse weeks–months later.
- Blood-stage prophylaxis (mefloquine, atovaquone-proguanil, doxycycline, chloroquine) does NOT kill hypnozoites.
- Only 8-aminoquinolines (primaquine, tafenoquine) eradicate hypnozoites → radical cure.
- Both contraindicated in pregnancy → defer to postpartum.
- Postpartum: confirm normal maternal G6PD → primaquine 14 days OR tafenoquine single dose (G6PD-confirmed normal, non-pregnant).
- Fetal G6PD cannot be reliably determined in utero → risk of hemolysis.
