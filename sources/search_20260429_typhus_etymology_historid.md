## Sources Checked

1. Online Etymology Dictionary, `typhus`
   URL: https://www.etymonline.com/word/typhus
   Key details: `typhus` entered medical Latin from Greek `typhos`, meaning feverish stupor and literally smoke. The entry notes older names including camp, jail, hospital, and ship fever, and explains that `typhoid` originally meant typhus-like.

2. National Library of Medicine, Polish Typhus Relief Commission: Typhus ward in Grace Hospital, Warsaw, Poland
   URL: https://collections.nlm.nih.gov/catalog/nlm:nlmuid-101395239-img
   Key details: public-domain historical photograph used as the HistorID hero image. The NLM record describes it as a typhus ward in Grace Hospital, Warsaw, Poland, from the Polish Typhus Relief Commission.

3. CDC, About Epidemic Typhus
   URL: https://www.cdc.gov/typhus/about/epidemic.html
   Key details: epidemic typhus is caused by `Rickettsia prowazekii`, transmitted through infected body lice, and associated with overcrowding.

4. CDC, About Flea-borne Typhus
   URL: https://www.cdc.gov/typhus/about/murine.html
   Key details: flea-borne typhus is caused by `Rickettsia typhi` and spread through contact with infected fleas, commonly Oriental rat fleas and cat fleas.

5. CDC, About Typhoid Fever and Paratyphoid Fever
   URL: https://www.cdc.gov/typhoid-fever/about/index.html
   Key details: typhoid fever is caused by `Salmonella` serotype Typhi and spreads through contaminated food, drinks, water, and human waste.
