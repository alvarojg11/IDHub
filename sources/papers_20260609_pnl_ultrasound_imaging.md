# Pure Neuritic Leprosy: Nerve Ultrasound & Imaging Diagnosis Papers
## Literature Search — 2026-06-09
### Query: "nerve ultrasound high-resolution sonography leprosy neuropathy"

---

## 1. Roy U, Panwar A, Cartwright MS, Srivastava AK, Mukherjee B, Vaibhaw H, et al. High Resolution Nerve Ultrasound in the Diagnosis of Pure Neuritic Leprosy.
- **Journal:** Journal of Clinical Ultrasound (JCU)
- **Year:** 2025 (Nov-Dec)
- **DOI:** 10.1002/jcu.24081
- **PMID:** 40457577
- **Key Findings:** Key recent paper comparing clinical, electrophysiologic, and sonographic characteristics of commonly affected peripheral nerves in suspected PNL. Describes an ultrasound-guided fine needle aspiration cytology (FNAC) procedure to improve diagnostic capabilities in PNL. Retrospective study from India (Oct 2022–Jun 2024).

## 2. Gunawan H, Khairuddin R, Hidayah RMN, Nugraha HG, Kuntara A, Amalia F. Peripheral Nerve Ultrasound Findings in Leprosy: A Scoping Review.
- **Journal:** Clinical, Cosmetic and Investigational Dermatology
- **Year:** 2026
- **DOI:** 10.2147/CCID.S588595
- **PMID:** 41869429
- **Key Findings:** Scoping review across 15 studies. Ultrasound offers non-invasive method to detect neuropathy by assessing echogenicity, cross-sectional area (CSA), and peripheral nerve vascularity. Synthesizes evidence for nerve ultrasound as diagnostic tool in leprosy.

## 3. De Martino Luppi A, Ferreira GE, Borges IS, Antunes DE, Araújo L, Dos Santos DF, et al. Role of multisegmental nerve ultrasound in the diagnosis of leprosy neuropathy.
- **Journal:** PLoS ONE
- **Year:** 2024
- **DOI:** 10.1371/journal.pone.0305808
- **PMID:** 39024363
- **Key Findings:** Assessed US measurements of cross-sectional areas (CSAs) of ulnar, median, and tibial nerves at osteofibrous tunnel and proximal points. Demonstrates multisegmental nerve thickening patterns characteristic of leprosy neuropathy.

## 4. Nagappa M, Pujar GS, Keshavan AH, Bathala L, Jain RD, Das A, et al. Sonographic pattern of median nerve enlargement in Hansen's neuropathy.
- **Journal:** Acta Neurologica Scandinavica
- **Year:** 2021 (Aug)
- **DOI:** 10.1111/ane.13432
- **PMID:** 33899225
- **Key Findings:** Median nerve enlargement in leprosy is more proximal than in carpal tunnel syndrome (CTS). This proximal enlargement pattern is a key sonographic discriminator between leprosy neuropathy and CTS — critical for differential diagnosis.

## 5. Nasr-Eldin YK, Cartwright MS, Hamed A, Ali LH, Abdel-Nasser AM. Exploration of Neurofilament Light Chain and Nerve Ultrasound in Leprotic Neuropathy.
- **Journal:** Journal of Ultrasound in Medicine
- **Year:** 2023 (Dec)
- **DOI:** 10.1002/jum.16319
- **PMID:** 37661825
- **Key Findings:** Combines serum neurofilament light chain (NfL) biomarker with nerve ultrasound findings. Correlates NfL levels with ultrasonographic nerve changes in leprosy, offering a dual diagnostic approach.

## 6. Voltan G, Marques-Júnior W, Santana JM, et al. Silent peripheral neuropathy determined by high-resolution ultrasound among contacts of patients with Hansen's disease.
- **Journal:** Frontiers in Medicine
- **Year:** 2022
- **DOI:** 10.3389/fmed.2022.1059448
- **PMID:** 36733931
- **Key Findings:** HRUS can detect silent neuropathy in household contacts of leprosy patients. Neural thickening detected by ultrasound may precede clinical symptoms, supporting screening role.

## 7. Wheat SW, Stryjewska B, Cartwright MS. A Hand-Held Ultrasound Device for the Assessment of Peripheral Nerves in Leprosy.
- **Journal:** Journal of Neuroimaging
- **Year:** 2021 (Jan)
- **DOI:** 10.1111/jon.12797
- **PMID:** 33176039
- **Key Findings:** Demonstrates feasibility of point-of-care handheld ultrasound for peripheral nerve assessment in leprosy — relevant for resource-limited settings.

## 8. Wang Z, Liu DY, Lei YY, Yang Z, Wang W. Ulnar nerve sonography in leprosy neuropathy.
- **Journal:** Journal of Medical Ultrasonics
- **Year:** 2016 (Jan)
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s10396-015-0652-2
- **PMID:** 26703181
- **Key Findings:** Characterizes ulnar nerve sonographic features in leprosy, including CSA enlargement and echotexture changes.

## 9. Y MR, Pidaparthi L, Tourani V, Penneru A, Murthy J. High-resolution ultrasound features of greater auricular nerve in leprosy.
- **Journal:** Postgraduate Medical Journal
- **Year:** 2020 (Jul)
- **DOI:** 10.1136/postgradmedj-2020-137703
- **PMID:** 32229512
- **Key Findings:** Describes HRUS features of the greater auricular nerve in leprosy, an accessible nerve for sonographic screening.

## 10. Dalave K, Patil P, Raman T, Agarwal U. Unmasking Carpal Tunnel Syndrome by High-Resolution Ultrasonography in Misdiagnosed Pure Neuritic Leprosy.
- **Journal:** Cureus
- **Year:** 2024 (Jul)
- **DOI:** 10.7759/cureus.65578
- **PMID:** 39192940
- **Key Findings:** Case report where HRUS correctly re-diagnosed bilateral CTS in a patient initially misdiagnosed with PNL. Demonstrates the critical role of HRUS in differential diagnosis.

## 11. Lugão HB, Frade MA, Mazzer N, Foss NT, Nogueira-Barbosa MH. Leprosy with ulnar nerve abscess: ultrasound findings in a child.
- **Journal:** Skeletal Radiology
- **Year:** 2017 (Jan)
- **DOI:** 10.1007/s00256-016-2517-1
- **PMID:** 27785545
- **Key Findings:** Ultrasound demonstration of nerve abscess formation in leprosy — important imaging finding.
