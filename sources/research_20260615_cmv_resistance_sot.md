# CMV Resistance in SOT — Research Sources

## Case: cmv-resistance-sot
## Date: 2026-06-15

---

## Key Clinical Facts Verified

### Epidemiology
- Lung transplant = highest CMV disease risk among all SOT
- D+/R- serostatus = highest-risk mismatch
- Late CMV disease occurs even after 12 months prophylaxis (standard for lung D+/R-)
- Ganciclovir resistance incidence: ~5-10% in SOT, higher in D+/R- and lung transplant

### Resistance Mechanisms

**UL97 Kinase Mutations (85-90% of resistance)**
- Common codons: M460V/I, A594V, L595S, C603W
- Prevent initial phosphorylation of ganciclovir
- Do NOT confer resistance to foscarnet or cidofovir (bypass UL97)
- Do NOT typically confer maribavir resistance (different binding site)
- A594V: ~5-10x IC50 shift, clinically significant

**UL54 DNA Polymerase Mutations (10-15% of resistance)**
- Confer broader cross-resistance depending on specific mutation
- Some: ganciclovir + cidofovir resistance
- Others: ganciclovir + foscarnet resistance
- Some: pan-resistance to ganciclovir + cidofovir + foscarnet

**Maribavir Resistance Mutations**
- T409M (most common)
- H411Y/N/L substitutions
- Located in ATP-binding region of UL97
- Distinct from ganciclovir resistance mutations

### Drug Mechanisms
- **Ganciclovir/valganciclovir**: Nucleoside analog, requires UL97 phosphorylation → UL97 mutations = resistance
- **Foscarnet**: Pyrophosphate analog, directly inhibits DNA polymerase, NO phosphorylation needed
- **Cidofovir**: Nucleotide phosphonate, has phosphonate group, phosphorylated by HOST kinases
- **Maribavir**: Benzimidazole L-riboside, INHIBITS UL97 kinase (doesn't need it for activation)
- **Letermovir**: Terminase complex inhibitor (UL56/UL89/UL51), prophylaxis only, NOT treatment

### Foscarnet Toxicity
- Nephrotoxicity: crystalline nephropathy, acute tubular necrosis
- Electrolyte wasting: hypokalemia, hypocalcemia, hypomagnesemia (tubular cation chelation)
- Genital ulceration from urinary excretion
- CNS symptoms
- Synergistic nephrotoxicity with calcineurin inhibitors

### Maribavir / SOLSTICE Trial
- FDA approved November 2021
- SOLSTICE: Phase 3, open-label, randomized (maribavir 400mg BID vs investigator-initiated therapy)
- 352 transplant recipients with refractory/resistant CMV
- Primary endpoint: CMV DNA clearance at Week 8 maintained through Week 16
- Results: 55.7% (maribavir) vs 23.9% (IIT)
- Oral formulation, no renal dose adjustment
- Common AEs: taste disturbance (~47%), nausea, diarrhea
- Paradoxical viral load increases in first weeks (mechanism: inhibits egress, not DNA replication)
- Activity retained against most UL97 ganciclovir-resistance mutations

### CMV Syndrome Definition (Ljungman 2017)
- CMV DNAemia detected
- Plus ≥2 of: fever ≥38°C, new neutropenia/thrombocytopenia, constitutional symptoms
- No evidence of end-organ tissue-invasive disease

## Differentiation from Existing Cases

- **cmv-colitis**: Kidney transplant, tissue-invasive (colitis), Q3 briefly covers resistance
- **cmv-alemtuzumab**: Non-transplant setting, CMV reactivation during lymphocyte depletion
- **acyclovir-resistant-hsv**: HSV resistance (UL23 thymidine kinase), different virus and mechanism
- **This case**: Lung transplant, CMV syndrome (no tissue disease), ALL 3 Qs about resistance, deeper dive into UL97/UL54 genotyping, foscarnet toxicity → maribavir arc
