# HistorID Source Notes: The Vaccine That Moved into the Bladder

Date: 2026-05-04

## BCG Vaccine History

Source: Lange C, Aaby P, Behr MA, Donald PR, Kaufmann SHE, Netea MG, Mandalakas AM. 100 years of Mycobacterium bovis bacille Calmette-Guerin. Lancet Infect Dis. 2022;22(1):e2-e12.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S1473-3099(21)00403-5

Key evidence used:

- BCG is derived from `Mycobacterium bovis`.
- It was first administered to a newborn baby in Paris in 1921.
- It remains the only licensed tuberculosis vaccine in humans.
- Its legacy extends beyond TB prevention into immunotherapy for malignancy, especially non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer.

Local metadata file: `sources/pubmed_20260504_bcg_vaccine_history.json`.

Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Bacille Calmette-Guerin (BCG) Vaccine for Tuberculosis. Updated January 31, 2025.

URL: https://www.cdc.gov/tb/hcp/vaccines/index.html

Key evidence used:

- BCG is not generally used in the United States for routine TB prevention.
- It protects children against severe forms of TB such as meningitis and miliary disease in higher-burden settings.
- BCG can complicate TB skin-test interpretation, which is part of its persistent modern ID footprint.

## Bladder Cancer Pivot

Source: Morales A, Eidinger D, Bruce AW. Intracavitary Bacillus Calmette-Guerin in the treatment of superficial bladder tumors. J Urol. 1976;116(2):180-183.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-5347(17)58737-6

Key evidence: This is the critical hinge for the second half of the story. Morales and colleagues treated patients with recurrent superficial bladder tumors using vesical and intradermal BCG and reported a favorable change in recurrence pattern in 9 patients.

Local metadata file: `sources/pubmed_20260504_bcg_bladder_history.json`.

Source: Singh AK, Srikrishna G, Bivalacqua TJ, Bishai WR. Recombinant BCGs for tuberculosis and bladder cancer. Vaccine. 2021;39(50):7321-7331.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.vaccine.2021.09.040

Key evidence used:

- BCG is an attenuated live strain of `Mycobacterium bovis`.
- Intravesical BCG is the only approved microbial immunotherapy for any form of cancer.
- It remains first-line therapy for treatment-naive non-muscle-invasive bladder cancer.

## Complication and ID Relevance

Source: Lange C, Aaby P, Behr MA, Donald PR, Kaufmann SHE, Netea MG, Mandalakas AM. 100 years of Mycobacterium bovis bacille Calmette-Guerin. Lancet Infect Dis. 2022;22(1):e2-e12.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S1473-3099(21)00403-5

Key evidence: The review explicitly notes that, as a live attenuated vaccine, BCG can cause localized or disseminated infection in immunocompromised hosts and can also do so after intravesical installation for bladder cancer.

## Verified Details Used

- The cleanest historical arc is 1921 to 1976: first human BCG use, then the Morales bladder-cancer paper.
- The article should not over-explain TB vaccine policy. It only needs enough vaccine context to make the bladder pivot feel surprising.
- The phrase "only approved microbial immunotherapy for any cancer" is strong and memorable, but should be attributed to the review rather than presented as free-floating fact.
- ID relevance should include not just immunotherapy success, but the fact that intravesical BCG can produce real mycobacterial complications.
