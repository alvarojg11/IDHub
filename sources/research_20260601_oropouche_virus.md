## Oropouche Virus Research Notes

Date: 2026-06-01
Slug: `oropouche-virus`
Title: `The Bite You Didn't Feel`

### Clinical anchors used for the case

- Syndrome: abrupt febrile illness with severe headache, retroorbital pain, myalgias, arthralgias, and faint maculopapular rash
- Geography: Amazon basin, especially Manaus / northern Brazil
- Differential: dengue, chikungunya, Zika, malaria, leptospirosis
- Primary vector: biting midges, especially `Culicoides paraensis`
- Diagnostics: RT-PCR is most useful during acute illness; serology is more retrospective
- Treatment: supportive care only
