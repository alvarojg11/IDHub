## Verified Details Used

- The 1977 Steere paper studied 51 residents in three contiguous Connecticut communities, including 39 children and 12 adults.
- The syndrome involved recurrent asymmetric swelling and pain in a few large joints, especially the knee.
- New cases peaked in summer and early fall.
- Some patients had an expanding annular rash before arthritis.
- The 1982 Science paper detected and isolated a treponema-like spirochete from adult Ixodes dammini and found patient sera reactive by indirect immunofluorescence.
