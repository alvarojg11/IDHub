# Influenza HSCT case notes

- Host selected: allogeneic HSCT recipient with GVHD on tacrolimus and prednisone; final case uses a woman patient to match the selected chest radiograph.
- Educational focus: influenza entry into respiratory epithelium, neuraminidase inhibitor mechanism, and prevention via antigenic drift plus close-contact protection.
- Figure path reserved for user-supplied chest radiograph: `public/cases/influenza-a-hsct/xray.png`.

## Content decisions

- Q1 tests HA-sialic acid binding, endosomal fusion, and M2-mediated uncoating.
- Q2 tests oseltamivir as a neuraminidase inhibitor and contrasts it with baloxavir and adamantanes.
- Q3 tests antigenic drift as the mechanistic basis for annual vaccine reformulation and reinforces layered prevention in immunocompromised hosts.
