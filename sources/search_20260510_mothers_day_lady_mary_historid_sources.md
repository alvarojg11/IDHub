# Lady Mary Mother's Day HistorID source notes

## Sources checked

1. Encyclopaedia Britannica, `Lady Mary Wortley Montagu`
   URL: https://www.britannica.com/biography/Lady-Mary-Wortley-Montagu
   Key details: confirms her severe smallpox illness and the broad claim that she helped pioneer inoculation in England after seeing the practice in Turkey.

2. Historic UK, `Lady Mary Wortley Montagu and Her Campaign Against Smallpox`
   URL: https://www.historic-uk.com/HistoryUK/HistoryofBritain/Lady-Mary-Wortley-Montagu-Campaign-Against-Smallpox/
   Key details: useful narrative source for the maternal angle. States that she had her son Edward inoculated in Constantinople by Charles Maitland and later had her daughter inoculated in London in 1721 during an epidemic.

3. Grundy I. `Montagu's variolation`. `Endeavour`. 2000;24(1):4-7.
   URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10824437/
   Key details: historically oriented medical article specifically centered on Montagu and variolation.

4. Weiss RA, Esparza J. `The prevention and eradication of smallpox: a commentary on Sloane (1755) "An account of inoculation"`. `Philos Trans R Soc Lond B Biol Sci`. 2015;370(1666):20140378.
   URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4360126/
   Key details: places Lady Mary in the broader smallpox prevention timeline and clearly distinguishes inoculation/variolation from Jenner's later vaccination.

5. Wellcome Collection, `Lady Mary Wortley Montagu. Stipple engraving by Caroline Watson, 1803.`
   URL: https://wellcomecollection.org/works/bw9udahc
   Image candidate URL checked: https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/V0004079/full/1200,/0/default.jpg
   Key details: usable portrait source for a Mother’s Day HistorID hero image. Wellcome site content is generally CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise noted.
